🛡️ CSEA Safety Standards

Our Commitment Against Child Sexual Exploitation and Abuse

CHILD PROTECTION PRIORITY

Statement of Principles

At WarnApp, the safety of minors is our top priority. We maintain a zero-tolerance stance against any form of child sexual exploitation and abuse (CSEA), including child sexual abuse material (CSAM).

⚠️ WARNING: WarnApp is a vehicle alert application designed exclusively for communication about any anomaly or incident that occurs on a registered vehicle. It is suitable for all audiences. We do not tolerate any type of content related to minors.

This document establishes the standards, policies, and procedures we implement to prevent, detect, and respond to any CSEA-related content or activity on our platform.

1. Prevention Policies

1.1 Content Limitation

  • Specific purpose: WarnApp only allows sharing alerts about incidents observed on specific vehicles to notify their owner:
    • Vehicle damage or impacts: For example, body scratch, dent, parking lot collision
    • Open doors or compartments: For example, door left open, trunk open, window rolled down
    • Activated lights or systems: For example, lights left on, alarm sounding, engine running
    • Risk or security situations: For example, theft attempt, vehicle being towed, flat tire
  • Absolute prohibition of:
    • Images, videos, or content of minors
    • Sexual content of any kind
    • Private messages or communications between users
    • Public profiles with sensitive personal information

1.2 Safe Design

Our application is designed from its architecture to minimize risks:

  • No direct messaging: No chat or private messaging system between users exists
  • Contextual alerts: Alerts can only be created for specific traffic locations
  • Preventive moderation: Strict limits on characters and multimedia content types
  • Anonymization: Users cannot search or directly contact other users

2. Detection Systems

2.1 Automated Moderation

🤖 AI Analysis

Automatic analysis of all uploaded images and videos using inappropriate content detection algorithms before publication.

🔍 PhotoDNA/Hash

Integration with known CSAM hash databases (Microsoft PhotoDNA, NCMEC Hash Database) for instant blocking.

📝 Text Filters

Automatic filtering of keywords, phrases, and patterns associated with child exploitation in alert messages.

⚡ Immediate Block

Any suspicious content is automatically blocked and quarantined for urgent manual review.

2.2 Human Review

  • Moderation team: Trained personnel review reported content or flagged by automated systems
  • Escalation protocol: Suspicious content is reviewed within 1 hour by senior moderators
  • Continuous training: Regular team training in CSAM and grooming identification
  • Team welfare: Psychological support protocols for moderators exposed to traumatic content

2.3 User Reporting System

  • Easy reporting: Visible report button on each alert with specific categories
  • Protected anonymity: Reports are completely anonymous
  • Maximum priority: Reports of minor-related content have maximum review priority
  • Feedback: Confirmation to reporting user of actions taken (without revealing sensitive details)

3. Response Procedures

3.1 Immediate Action Protocol

When CSEA-related suspicious content is detected or reported, we immediately activate:

  1. Immediate removal: Content is deleted from all servers as soon as it is detected
  2. Account suspension: Immediate and permanent blocking of the offending user
  3. Evidence preservation: Forensic capture of data for legal investigation
  4. Authority notification: Mandatory report to competent authorities
  5. Network review: Analysis of possible related accounts or abuse networks

3.2 Collaboration with Authorities

📋 Legal commitment: WarnApp fully cooperates with police and judicial authorities in all countries where we operate. We provide complete evidence and cooperate in investigations without delay.

  • NCMEC reports: Mandatory notification to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) in the US
  • Europol/Interpol: Collaboration with international networks fighting CSAM
  • Local authorities: Report to national police and specialized prosecutors in each jurisdiction
  • Response time: Reports sent within 24 hours of detection
  • Data preservation: Digital evidence retention according to legal requirements of each country

3.3 Bans and Recidivism Prevention

  • Permanent ban: Lifetime platform usage prohibition
  • Device blacklist: Device identifiers permanently blocked
  • Email/phone blocking: Impossibility to create new accounts with same data
  • Hash sharing: We report CSAM content hashes to global databases
  • Watchlist: Monitoring suspicious registration attempt patterns

4. Transparency and Accountability

4.1 Transparency Reports

We will publish annual transparency reports (starting 2027) that include:

  • Number of CSEA-related content removed
  • Permanently suspended accounts
  • Reports sent to authorities
  • Improvements implemented in detection systems
  • Average incident response time

4.2 External Audits

  • Independent review: Annual audits of our child protection systems
  • Certifications: Adherence to international digital safety standards
  • Continuous improvement: Implementation of child protection expert recommendations

4.3 Education and Awareness

  • User guides: Clear information about what to report and how
  • NGO collaboration: Joint work with child protection organizations
  • Internal training: All WarnApp team receives annual child protection training

5. Implemented Technologies

💡 Security investment: WarnApp invests significant resources in cutting-edge technology for child protection, including AI tools, hash databases, and automated moderation systems.

  • Google Cloud Vision API: Inappropriate content detection in images
  • Microsoft PhotoDNA: Comparison with known CSAM database
  • NCMEC Hash Database: Verification against illegal content hashes
  • Amazon Rekognition: Additional visual content analysis
  • Content Safety AI: Textual and multimedia content classification
  • Firebase ML Kit: On-device analysis before server upload
  • Reputation system: Algorithm identifying suspicious behaviors

6. Corporate Commitment

WarnApp is committed to:

🎯 Zero Tolerance

Maintain a strict zero-tolerance policy against any form of CSEA on our platform.

🚀 Continuous Innovation

Adopt and implement the latest technologies and best practices in child protection.

🤝 Global Collaboration

Work closely with authorities, NGOs, and other technology platforms in the fight against CSEA.

📊 Total Transparency

Be transparent about our efforts, results, and areas for improvement in child protection.

7. Legal Compliance

WarnApp complies with all international laws and regulations related to child protection, including:

  • COPPA (US): Children's Online Privacy Protection Act
  • GDPR (EU): General Data Protection Regulation (special protection for minors)
  • Organic Law 1/1996 (Spain): Legal Protection of Minors
  • Law 26.904 (Argentina): Grooming or cyber harassment
  • Law nº 12.015/2009 (Brazil): Crimes against sexual dignity
  • Child Safety Online (UK): Online Safety Act 2023
  • Digital Services Act (EU): Illegal content moderation obligations

⚖️ Legal liability: Distribution, possession, or creation of child sexual abuse material is a serious crime in all countries. Offenders will be reported to authorities and may face prison sentences of up to 20 years depending on jurisdiction.
